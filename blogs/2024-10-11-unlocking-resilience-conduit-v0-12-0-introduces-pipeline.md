The Conduit team has just released Conduit v0.12, and we're gearing up for the launch of Conduit v1 with a focus on making pipelines more resilient. One key feature of this release is pipeline recovery, designed to automatically restart pipelines that experience temporary errors like network interruptions or service downtime. With configurable backoff settings, Conduit can efficiently handle retries, reducing the impact of transient issues.
